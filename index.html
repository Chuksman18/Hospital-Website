PHARMACY

Our in-house pharmacy provides safe, accurate and timely dispensing of medications. Our licensed pharmacists work closely with doctors to ensure the right treatment for every patient. We stock a wide range of prescription medicines, over-the-counter products, and specialised formulations, with clear counselling on dosage and side effects.

DENTIST

Our dental unit offers complete oral healthcare for the whole family. From routine check-ups and cleanings to fillings, root canal treatment, extractions, and cosmetic dentistry, our experienced dentists use modern equipment in a calm and hygienic environment to give you a healthy, confident smile.

OPTICIAN

Our eye care department provides comprehensive vision services including eye examinations, prescription of spectacles and contact lenses, treatment of common eye conditions, and specialist ophthalmology consultations. We help protect and improve your sight with accurate diagnosis and modern equipment.

GYNAECOLOGY

Our Gynaecology department provides specialised care for women’s health at every stage of life. Services include routine check-ups, pregnancy care, family planning, treatment of menstrual disorders, menopause management, and minor gynaecological procedures in a private and supportive environment.

THEATRE

Our fully equipped operating theatres support a wide range of surgical procedures. From minor day-case operations to complex surgeries, our skilled surgical teams work with modern technology and strict infection-control standards to ensure patient safety and excellent outcomes.

CONSULTANTS

Our team of specialist consultants brings years of experience across multiple medical fields. Patients benefit from expert assessment, accurate diagnosis, and personalised treatment plans. Consultants work closely with other departments to deliver coordinated, high-quality care.

RESEARCH LAB

Our modern laboratory offers accurate and timely diagnostic testing. From routine blood work and urine analysis to specialised investigations, our laboratory supports doctors with reliable results that guide effective treatment decisions.

CHILDCARE CENTER

Our paediatric and childcare services are designed especially for infants, children and adolescents. Friendly paediatricians provide well-child checks, vaccinations, treatment of childhood illnesses, and developmental assessments in a child-friendly environment.

INVENTORY

Our efficient inventory system ensures that essential medicines, medical supplies and equipment are always available. Careful stock management supports uninterrupted patient care and maintains the highest standards of quality and safety.

Our Services

Emergency Care

Our Emergency Department is open 24/7 and ready to handle urgent medical situations. Highly trained staff provide rapid assessment and stabilisation for trauma, heart attacks, strokes, severe infections and other life-threatening conditions.

Cardiology

Our Cardiology unit specialises in the diagnosis and treatment of heart and blood vessel conditions. Services include ECG, echocardiography, stress testing, management of hypertension, heart failure, arrhythmias, and preventive heart-care programmes.

Orthopaedics

Our Orthopaedic department treats conditions affecting bones, joints, muscles and ligaments. From fractures and sports injuries to arthritis and joint replacement surgery, our orthopaedic specialists help patients regain mobility and reduce pain.

Radiology and Imaging

Our Radiology and Imaging department uses advanced technology (X-ray, ultrasound, CT and other imaging modalities) to provide clear, detailed pictures that help doctors make accurate diagnoses. Fast reporting supports timely treatment decisions.

Physiotherapy

Our Physiotherapy unit helps patients recover strength, mobility and function after injury, surgery or illness. Individualised treatment plans may include exercise therapy, manual therapy, electrotherapy and rehabilitation programmes for all age groups.

Do I need an appointment to visit?

While walk ins are welcome for emergencies we recommend booking an appointment for outpatient consultations to reduce waiting time.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes we accept all major health insurance providers including NHIS and private HMOs. Contact our billing department for details.

What are your emergency services hours?

Our emergency department operates 24 hours a day 7 days a week including public holidays.

How long does a consultation take?

A standard consultation takes between 20 and 45 minutes depending on the nature of your visit.

Can I request a specific doctor?

Yes you can request a specific doctor when booking your appointment subject to their availability on your preferred date.
